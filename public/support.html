Install Lite on your phone

Open lite.sovereignqr.com, then add it to your Home Screen (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen). After the first visit it works with no network.

The camera doesn't start

Allow camera access for the site in your browser's settings, then reload. You can also scan a picture of a code.

Lite says to open a code in the full app

That code holds data or encryption only Sovereign QR can read. The full app is coming to iPhone first.

A new version is ready

Tap Reload when Lite asks. Your saved codes stay.
