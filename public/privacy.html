Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026.

In short

Your codes are made and read on your device. We don't collect them, and we don't track you.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no forms.
  • It is hosted on Cloudflare, which handles technical request data (such as your IP address) to deliver and protect the site. We see only overall traffic numbers.

Sovereign QR Lite

  • Codes are made and read on your device. What you type or scan is never sent anywhere.
  • The camera is used only while you scan, and its picture stays on your device.
  • Codes you save to the home page stay in your browser's storage on that device. Clearing the site's data removes them.
  • Loading and updating the app goes through Cloudflare, as for this website.
